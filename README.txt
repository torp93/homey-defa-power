See what your DEFA charger is doing and take control of it from Homey. Live charging power, energy for the session and for the lifetime of the charger, and the state of the cable and the car — all as normal Homey values you can build Flows on. Start and stop charging, switch eco mode, charge now instead of waiting for the smart schedule, and restart the charger when it needs it.

There is more if you let the app talk to the charger on your own network. Then you can also set the charging current from a Flow, so charging follows your electricity price or backs off when the rest of the house is drawing a lot, and you get switches for Plug & Charge and offline charging. The app signs in with your own DEFA account and is not made by DEFA.
